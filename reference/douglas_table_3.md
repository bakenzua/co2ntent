# Table 3 - 38 arterial and venous blood gas measurements.

A dataset containing 38 venous and arterial blood gas measurements from
five patients. Presented as table 3 in (Douglas et al. 1988) from the
work of (Loeppky et al. 1983)

## Usage

``` r
douglas_table_3
```

## Format

A data frame with 38 rows and 8 variables:

- subject:

  subject, of five participants

- sample_type:

  type of sample, arterial or venous

- ph:

  ph, hydrogen ion concentration

- haemoglobin_g_dl:

  haemoglobin, in grams per decilitre

- so2_fraction:

  haemoglobin oxygen saturation as a fraction, ie between 0 and 1

- blood_co2_content_ml_dl:

  CO2 content of blood in mls per decilitre

- pco2_torr:

  partial pressure of CO2 in torr(mmHg)

- plasma_co2_content_ml_dl:

  CO2 content of plasma in mls per decilitre

## References

Douglas AR, Jones NL, Reed JW (1988). “Calculation of whole blood CO2
content.” *J. Appl. Physiol.*, **65**(1), 473–477. Loeppky JA, Luft UC,
Fletcher ER (1983). “Quantitative description of whole blood CO2
dissociation curve and Haldane effect.” *Respir Physiol*, **51**(2),
167–181.
