# Table 4 - 46 arterial blood gas measurements.

A dataset containing 46 arterial blood gas measurements from four
patients. Presented as table 4 in (Douglas et al. 1988) from the work of
(Jones et al. 1971)

## Usage

``` r
douglas_table_4
```

## Format

A data frame with 48 rows and 8 variables:

- subject:

  subject, of four participants

- po2_torr:

  partial pressure of O2 in torr(mmHg), contains some non numeric
  entries!

- ph:

  ph, hydrogen ion concentration

- haemoglobin_g_dl:

  haemoglobin, in grams per decilitre

- so2_fraction:

  haemoglobin oxygen saturation as a fraction, ie between 0 and 1

- blood_co2_content_ml_dl:

  CO2 content of blood in mls per decilitre

- pco2_torr:

  partial pressure of CO2 in torr(mmHg)

- plasma_co2_content_ml_dl:

  CO2 content of plasma in mls per decilitre

## References

Douglas AR, Jones NL, Reed JW (1988). “Calculation of whole blood CO2
content.” *J. Appl. Physiol.*, **65**(1), 473–477. Jones NL, Levine GB,
Robertson DG, Epstein SW (1971). “The effect of added dead space on the
pulmonary response to exercise.” *Respiration*, **28**(5), 389–398.
